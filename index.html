📚 ECE Theory: Parallel vs. Serial Communication

Key Characteristics of Parallel Interface

  • Simultaneous Transmission: All 8 bits can be transferred at the same time using separate data lines.
  • Handshaking: Control signals such as STROBE, BUSY and ACK help synchronize the transfer.
  • High Throughput: Multiple bits can be transferred in one communication cycle.

Design Trade-offs & Limitations

  • Clock Skew: Different propagation delays can affect timing.
  • Crosstalk: Adjacent signal lines may interfere with each other.
  • Bulky Cabling: More physical data lines are required compared with single-line serial communication.
